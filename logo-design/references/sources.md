# Sources

## Curation sites (ranked, 2026-10-03)

1. Logobook (logobook.com): about 5,000 modernist marks as clean SVGs. Browse by designer, shape and object. Best for raw marks.
2. LogoArchive / Logo Archive Research, BP&O, Richard Baird (logo-archive.org, logohistories.com): the best editing of modernist work.
3. Chermayeff & Geismar & Haviv portfolio (cghnyc.com/work/logos): the best teacher of "simple, but an idea".
4. Logo Index (logo-index.com): research grade, with filters by shape, decade and designer.
5. LogoLounge (logolounge.com): the largest collection. Use it to check a concept for clichés (full search needs a paid account).
6. macOS / iOS Icon Gallery by Jim Nielsen (macosicongallery.com): app icons by designer.
7. icon.museum, appiconbook.com: small, well-edited app icon sets.
8. Brand New / UnderConsideration, Identity Designed: whole identity systems and the reasoning behind them.

## Courses

- Sagi Haviv, "Logo Design: From Concept to Presentation" (Domestika) and "Designing Brand Symbols" (Skillshare).
- Michael Flarup, "Designing App Icons" (applypixels, 14 lessons) and his App Icon Design Masterclass.
- Everton Gargioni, "Logo Design: Create Powerful Symbols" (Domestika): point, line, rotation, proximity, repetition.
- Chris Do, "Logo Design 01" (The Futur).
- Aaron Draplin, "Secrets of Shape, Type and Color" (Skillshare).
- Free: Apple HIG App icons; WWDC25 "Create icons with Icon Composer"; WWDC26 "Icon Composer for Beginners"; Bjango's Mac app icon workflow.

## Search engine notes

- **yt-dlp search** was the best way to find YouTube videos. Exa, Parallel and context.dev mostly returned beginner tutorials.
- **Exa** was best for primary texts and fresh news.
- **Parallel** was best for checking quotes and for course syllabi.
- **context.dev** was best for official pages and brand data (logos and brand colours by domain), and the only tool that rendered the Apple HIG page.
- **Reddit-only searches returned nothing** on every engine.
